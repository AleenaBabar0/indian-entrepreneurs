Company Setup in Dubai

Not every business fits into the same structure. Depending on your goals, target market, and budget, you can choose from three main business setup options in the UAE.

Mainland Company

100% Ownership Govt Clients Full Market Access

Ideal for businesses that want to trade across the UAE and work directly with government and local clients without restrictions.

Free Zone Company

Fast Setup Tax Exempt Industry-Specific

Perfect for startups and SMEs seeking 100% ownership, cost-effective setup, and simplified operations. Free Zones are well-suited for trading, e-commerce, consulting, and export-focused businesses, with faster setup timelines and industry-specific benefits.

Holding Company

Asset Protection Group Ownership Investment Holding

Best for investors who want to hold shares, protect assets, and manage multiple companies under one structure.

UAE Company Formation Process

A guided, end-to-end approach designed to make UAE company formation easy for Indian business owners.

01

Free Consultation & Business Activity Selection

We begin with a detailed consultation to understand your business idea, goals, budget, and expansion plans from India to the UAE. Based on this discussion, our experts recommend the most suitable business activity that aligns with UAE regulations. This ensures your license choice is correct from day one and avoids issues during approvals or banking.

1 / 6
02

Jurisdiction & License Selection

Once the activity is clear, we help you choose between Mainland, Free Zone, or Offshore, based on your target market, ownership needs, office requirements, and budget. We explain the license type, visa quota, and costs of each option so you can decide with full clarity and no hidden surprises.

2 / 6
03

Approvals & Documentation

Our team prepares and submits your application, handles the required initial and external approvals, and guides you on the documents needed from India, including attestation where applicable. You share the papers, we take care of the paperwork and follow-ups with the authorities.

3 / 6
04

Company Registration & Trade License

After approvals, we complete the company registration, process the government fees, and secure your trade license along with the establishment card. Your company is then legally ready to operate in the UAE, with all documents delivered to you in an organised set.

4 / 6
05

Visa Processing & Bank Account Support

We process your investor and employee visas, including medical tests and Emirates ID, and assist with opening a corporate bank account. With our experience in banking requirements for Indian-owned companies, we prepare your documentation so the process runs as smoothly as possible.

5 / 6
06

Ongoing Compliance & Renewals

Our support continues after setup. We assist with VAT and Corporate Tax registration, accounting, license and visa renewals, and regulatory filings, so your business stays compliant and you can focus on growth.
